About me

Who is ä?

Working for over twenty years in the action sports media, a world where anything can happen at any time, has given me a solid ability to adapt to any situation, request or need. Traveling the globe over the years has helped me build an approach based on open-mindedness, listening and sharing, whatever the project I’m planning.

With this wealth of experience and skills, I decided to combine them to create my own agency, called agenceä. The vast and complex world of Outdoor and Action Sports demands knowledge and expertise. That’s where agenceä comes in!

JP Mothes — agenceÄ founder

Why ä?

It is a tribute to the little village of Å, also known as Moskenes, in Norway’s Lofoten Islands. I was lucky enough to stay there during a shoot. Thanks to the magnificent simplicity of this place, hidden in the recesses of a fjord at the end of a road, and the harmony of its colors, I realized that when everything aligns, a magical moment of pure bliss takes place. I promised myself I would name my project after this place… welcome to ä!
